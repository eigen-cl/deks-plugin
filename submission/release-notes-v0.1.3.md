# DEKS v0.1.3

DEKS now separates safe product operation from an opinionated presentation-design method. Agents can inspect workspace assets, choose or complete cohesive palettes, work from one presentation motion beat, rely on slide entry and exit defaults for the base flow, reserve per-element motion for narrative exceptions, and audit geometry, DOM measurements, rendered checkpoints, and actual transitions as one continuous loop.

The operational guidance also honors internal server-enforced complexity bounds. Rejected writes remain atomic and never prune existing content implicitly; those capacities are not ordinary presentation or usage copy.
