# DEKS v0.1.4

DEKS now plans presentation motion section by section. Agents define the story
identities that persist in each section, keep those elements on stable anchors,
and re-anchor them only at deliberate scene boundaries or when the narrative
action requires movement.

Every slide edge names one central motion. Ordinary text fades, appears, or
remains in place, while travel and geometry interpolation are reserved for the
objects that explain the story. The audit guidance also flags unexplained
per-slide drift and requires rendered transition review before completion.
