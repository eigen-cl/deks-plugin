# Editorial quality and evidence

## Frame the assignment

Before mutating a deck, state:

- audience and their current knowledge;
- decision or behavior the presentation should cause;
- delivery format and approximate duration;
- narrative spine in one sentence;
- evidence standard and source date.

Do not make these five items into a slide unless they are part of the requested presentation.

## Keep a claim ledger

Track each material assertion as:

- **sourced fact** — supported by a current primary source;
- **inference** — reasoned from sources and labelled as such;
- **estimate** — method and uncertainty stated;
- **unresolved** — omit or visibly qualify before delivery.

Prefer official product documentation, contractual terms, standards bodies, laws, and original research. Record the retrieval date for provider policies, product capabilities, laws, prices, and other changing claims.

Do not convert a scoped statement into a universal one. In particular:

- distinguish personal, business, enterprise, API, and workspace plans;
- distinguish model training, request processing, retention, human review, and administrative audit;
- distinguish host hooks, MCP tools/resources, plugins, proxies, endpoint controls, DLP, and CASB;
- verify a claimed hook's exact host, event name, timing, payload, scope, and blocking behavior;
- describe web applications by the controls they actually expose, not as inherently governed or ungovernable.

## Cite visibly

DEKS currently has no native speaker notes or citation metadata. Put a concise source note on the relevant checkpoint or a visible sources checkpoint. Use a `link-button` only when a clickable HTTPS source helps the audience; its label and URL are part of the composition.

Keep citations legible without competing with the focal claim. If a claim cannot be sourced or fairly qualified, remove it.

## Close a proposal with a decision

When the presentation is meant to secure approval, end with the specific decision requested and a bounded pilot that will generate evidence for the next decision. Reuse values supplied by the user or supported by sources. Never make up adoption goals, time boxes, participant counts, budgets, or success thresholds to make the close look concrete; state qualitative acceptance criteria or mark the missing value as unresolved instead.
